Common Questions

Printer Service FAQs

Quick answers to common setup and troubleshooting questions.

Check that the Printer Services wireless networking, connect it to your home or office network, and make sure your computer or phone uses that same network. The exact steps vary by model.

Use the printer manufacturer's official support website and select your exact model and operating system. Avoid unfamiliar driver download sites.

Check printer power, cables or Wi-Fi, default-printer selection and the print queue. Restarting the printer and computer can sometimes clear a temporary connection problem.

Include the printer brand and model, your computer or phone type, the connection method, any error message, and what you have already tried.
